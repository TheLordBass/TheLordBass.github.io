About

Analysis that ends in a decision

Most analysis dies in a spreadsheet nobody opens twice. The part I care about is the last mile — framing the question properly, getting the data clean enough to trust, and presenting it so a non-technical stakeholder can make a call in the meeting rather than asking for another version.

Day to day that means a lot of SQL against relational databases, Python and Pandas for the cleaning and joining that would otherwise be manual, and Tableau or Power BI for anything that needs to be looked at more than once. I graduated from Middlesex University in 2021 with First Class Honours in Information Technology and Business Information Systems, and I've been working in analytics since.

I'm currently open to Business Analyst and Data Analyst roles — permanent or contract, Manchester-based or remote.
